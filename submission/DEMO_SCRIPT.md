# Demo script (2–3 minutes)

Live demo: https://misconception-detective-612071683004.asia-south1.run.app
Use a desktop browser window about 1280 px wide. Screenshots of each step are in `submission/screenshots/`.

If Gemini is temporarily unavailable, steps 4 and 6 still work with built-in help; say so honestly (see "If AI is unavailable").

## 0:00 – Open (15 s)
"This is Misconception Detective, a fractions tutor. It opens on **Solve my problem**: you type your own problem."
Click the **1/2 + 1/4** example chip. Point out *I read this as: Add 1/2 and 1/4*: "It shows how it understood me before it solves, so I can fix it."

## 0:15 – A wrong answer (30 s)
Type **2/6** in *Your answer* and press **Check my working**.
Show (screenshot `01`):
- The original problem is still visible beside the explanation.
- "Your answer 2/6" and "Correct answer 3/4" are separate tiles.
- The feedback says what it *observed* ("2/6 is smaller than 1/2, but adding can only make it bigger") and a *possible* reason, labelled "not a diagnosis".
- Steps: `1/2 = 2/4`, `2/4 + 1/4 = (2 + 1)/4 = 3/4`, with the reason "The denominator tells us the size of each piece… we keep the denominator 4 because the pieces are still quarters."

## 0:45 – Relevant, AI-selected help (45 s)
Scroll to **Ask about this problem**. Type **Why do the denominators have to match?** and press **Ask**.
Show (screenshot `02`): the card is labelled **AI-selected help: Gemini chose this explanation**, and the header badge now says *AI-selected help (Gemini)*.
Say: "Gemini only picked *which* explanation to show. The words and maths come from the app's verified engine, so the model can't state something false."
Then ask **Can you show me a picture?** and **Why is my answer wrong?**: each gives a different explanation (a picture of the step; a tentative possible mistake).
Optional: ask "What is the capital of France?" It is declined as out of scope.

## 1:30 – Similar problem (15 s)
Click **Give me a similar problem**. A different, solvable problem appears; the answer box is empty so the student tries it themselves.

## 1:45 – Practise (30 s)
Open **Practise**. Enter **7 / 3** (wrong). Show (screenshot `06`): the question stays on screen; **Try again**, **Give me a hint**, **Explain step by step** and **Give me a similar problem** are offered, and the next question appears only when the student chooses it.
Say: "Hints and viewed solutions never count as independent understanding."

## 2:15 – My progress (30 s)
Open **My progress** (screenshot `03`): "Only my real activity. This item is *Not yet correct*. There are no scores and no made-up data." Open **Review the explanation**, and point out **Delete my activity history** and **Settings**.

## 2:45 – Close (10 s)
"Not yet tested with students, so I make no learning claims. It's built to be honest about what is verified and what is AI."

## If AI is unavailable
Ask the same question: the card is labelled **Built-in help**, a simple message says AI help isn't available right now, and the header badge does *not* claim AI is working (screenshot `04`).
